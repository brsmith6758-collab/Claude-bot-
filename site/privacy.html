Privacy policy

What we collect and how we use it

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

When you request a cash offer we collect the property address and details you provide, your name, phone number and email address, your choice about text messages, and anything you add in the notes. With each request we keep a record of the consent wording you saw and when you sent it. The website and its hosting providers also see standard technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages you visit.

How we use it

We use your information to look at the property, prepare and explain a cash offer, and talk with you about a possible sale by phone or email, and by text only if you agreed to texts. We don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

Text messages

If you check the box to receive texts, we text you about your property and your offer. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP (or ALTO) to any text to stop, and HELP (or AYUDA) for help. We don't share mobile numbers or texting consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Stopping calls, texts or emails

You can ask us to stop at any time: reply STOP to a text, tell us in the website chat, or call or email us. We stop as soon as we can and within the time the law allows.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to complete a sale you agree to, for example with the title company or closing attorney, and with the service providers below that run this website and deliver our messages. If anyone other than Prime Acre Capital LLC would be the buyer of your property, we will tell you who before sharing your details with them.

Service providers

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form requests are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit. Our website chat runs on Cloudflare, which also handles our domain's DNS and email routing, and uses Anthropic's AI to write its replies. These providers process information only to provide their services to us.

Cookies and advertising

This website doesn't currently use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we turn any on, we'll list them here, with how to opt out.

Website chat

Our website chat is an AI assistant. What you type in the chat is processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) and our hosting provider (Cloudflare) so the assistant can reply, check open call times, and send the details you share to our team. Please don't share sensitive information such as Social Security, bank, or loan account numbers in the chat. Chat messages are not used to make decisions about you; a person from our team reviews every request you send through the chat. If you ask in the chat for us to stop calling, texting or emailing you, that request is passed to our team and honored. After you send your details, anything else you type in the chat is passed to our team as well.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time. We keep lead information only as long as needed to evaluate and complete a potential purchase and to meet legal requirements, including records of consent.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Prime Acre Capital LLC at brsmith6758@gmail.com.
